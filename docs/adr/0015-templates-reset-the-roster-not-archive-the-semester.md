# Templates reset the roster, not archive the semester

For #147 ("save a semester for reuse with new students") we considered two shapes: archive-and-recall, where the outgoing semester's Class — students, final progress, review flags and all — stays browsable later; or reset-and-discard, where only the curriculum survives and the old roster is simply gone. We chose reset-and-discard, implemented as a Template: a frozen, teacher-owned snapshot of Subjects and/or Lessons only, saved independent of the live Class/Subject it came from. Archive-and-recall would need a "past semesters" browsing surface the app doesn't have and today's single-class-per-teacher design doesn't call for; curriculum is already independent of Students/Progress in the schema, so a lighter save-the-shape model covers the actual need. Consequence: Templates are the only reuse mechanism — there's no separate "clear the roster" bulk action, and no history of past semesters to look back on. A teacher who wants a record of a semester before resetting it uses the existing per-student Reports screen manually; this issue doesn't automate that.
